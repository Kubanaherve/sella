## Short description

Sales, stock and credit ledger for shops in Rwanda.

## Full description

Sella is the register a shop uses while the customer is still there. Record the sale, see the stock, and keep Ideni, the credit book. The counter keeps selling if the internet drops, and the sale syncs when the connection returns.

A cashier signs in with a 4-digit PIN. Normal is one shop, the owner, and one cashier. Pro is up to three shops and five cashiers, with desktop, Excel or CSV import, PDF debt reports, and Ask the Shop.

Trial is free for 7 days, including Pro tools. Normal is 27,000 RWF a month or 270,000 RWF a year. Pro is 48,500 RWF a month or 485,000 RWF a year. A year is ten monthly payments, charged once. After a paid period, the shop keeps write access for 3 days.

Subscription payment stays on the website and uses MTN MoMo. The Play build must not sell the subscription inside the app.
