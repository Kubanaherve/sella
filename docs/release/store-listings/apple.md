## Subtitle

## Description

Sella records a sale, the stock, and Ideni while the customer is still at the counter. Sales continue on the phone when the network drops and sync when it returns. Cashiers use a 4-digit PIN.

Plans, in Rwandan francs: Trial free for 7 days; Normal 27,000 a month or 270,000 a year; Pro 48,500 a month or 485,000 a year. The App Store build must not sell the subscription inside the app. Purchase stays on the website.
