# Sella brand

Taken from `src/lib/brand.ts` and the public site. Do not invent a second palette.

## Color

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep blue | `#0B3FCC` | Primary actions, wordmark depth |
| Bright blue | `#1A6FFF` | Icon highlight |
| Accent | `#3B82F6` | Secondary blue |
| Ink | `#040816` | Near-black text and dark glass base |
| Sand | `#F3F7FF` | Light surface |
| Cyan | `#5AC8FA` | Glass edge light |
| Magenta | `#D033FF` | Rare accent in the icon, not a page color |

Public pages use near-black text, white surfaces, and fine gray borders. Blue glass is limited to the phone-frame edge, the active product tab, and payment confirmation.

## Type

Inter is the product typeface (`@fontsource/inter`). Outfit and Sora are installed in the app but Inter is the face used for the public system.
