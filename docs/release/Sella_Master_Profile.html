1. Executive overview

Sella is shop software by KUBANA Friend TECHNOLOGIES LTD. The legal name on the RDB certificate is KUBANA Friend TECHNOLOGIES LTD. The in-app constant in src/lib/company.ts reads KUBANA FRIEND TECHNOLOGIES. TIN 157374984. Registration number used on filings: REG-2026-939550.

The job is the counter. A sale, the stock left, and Ideni — the credit book — have to be true before the customer leaves. The phone keeps selling if the internet drops. The sale syncs when the connection returns. If two copies disagree, the owner reviews them before anything is overwritten.

Default public language is Kinyarwanda. The app also runs in English, French, Swahili, Kirundi, and Luganda. Chinese and Arabic are not product languages.

2. What the product actually does

At the counter

  • Ring a sale. Split the payment across cash, MTN MoMo, and Airtel Money.
  • Check a MoMo reference so the same code is not entered twice.
  • Cashier sign-in is a 4-digit PIN.
  • Receipt on a thermal printer, or a PDF. The receipt shows the shop, the lines, the cashier, and the amount.
  • Stock cards and low-stock alerts.

Ideni and the books

  • Open Ideni, see what was bought and what was paid, record a new payment, or send the balance on WhatsApp.
  • PDF debt reports. On Pro, the report can carry the shop pay number.
  • Excel and CSV import of products and customers, on Pro.
  • Ask the Shop, the in-app assistant named ChatSella, on Pro.

3. How a sale and a subscription move

1
Open the shop. The owner creates the shop. A cashier joins with the shop code and a 4-digit PIN.
2
Sell. The register writes the sale on the device. Local storage is Dexie (sella_local). Cash, MoMo, Airtel, or Ideni can share one sale.
3
Sync. When the network returns, the sale syncs. A conflict is shown to the owner instead of being overwritten in silence.
4
Subscribe, on the website only. WebMomoCheckout sends an MTN MoMo request-to-pay to a phone number of 8 to 15 digits. The page does not mark success by itself. paymentsApi.ts waits for a server SUCCESSFUL result and the amount must match 27,000, 270,000, 48,500, or 485,000 RWF.
5
Store apps. Android and iOS are Capacitor shells, id com.sella.app. They must hide checkout and purchase links. Desktop is an Electron window on the same web app. It is not a second product and it is not signed.

A live MoMo merchant account is not approved in this dossier. The KYC form and the MoMo Pay application are filled and filed in the legal folder. Production API credentials were empty when this file was written.

4. Who it is for

The first shop is in Kigali: one counter, an owner, and a cashier, selling in francs. Normal is that shop. Pro is the owner who runs up to three shops, wants a desktop window, and needs import plus debt PDFs.

The language set is East African on purpose: Kinyarwanda first, then English, French, Swahili, Kirundi, and Luganda. Growth means more of those shops on the same register, not a second product. Marketplace and store routes exist in the app. They are not the homepage story. The homepage leads with the register, stock, and Ideni.
