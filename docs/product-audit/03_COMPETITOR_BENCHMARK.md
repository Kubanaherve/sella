## Ten biggest competitive weaknesses

1. No staff roles / permissions.  
2. No robust offline↔cloud sync queue.  
3. Thin analytics vs Square/Shopify.  
4. No returns/exchanges UX.  
5. No barcode / variants depth.  
6. Design polish below premium POS.  
7. Cloud financial write safety immature.  
8. Hardware/card ecosystem absent.  
9. Onboarding/education weak.  
10. Store presence / trust brand missing.

## Ten strongest advantages

1. Customer credit as first-class.  
2. WhatsApp reminder + statement intent.  
3. RWF / Rwanda phone / Kinyarwanda.  
4. MoMo number on invoice (display).  
5. PIN-simple daily unlock.  
6. Sale-first unified order model.  
7. Affordable SaaS pricing intent (5k/50k).  
8. Works on phone without POS hardware.  
9. Local-first option for poor connectivity.  
10. Focused for small informal retail (not omnichannel bloat).

## Premium fintech design lessons (Revolut/Wise — patterns only)

- One hero money number.  
- Progressive disclosure.  
- Transparent fees/outcomes.  
- Confirm + reversible actions.  
- Trust signals near sensitive ops.  

Do **not** copy branding/assets.
